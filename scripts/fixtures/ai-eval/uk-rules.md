In the rules below, "translation" means the definition field, and example-sentence rules apply to exampleTranslation. The example field itself stays in the language being learned.

Style rules for Ukrainian card content:
- The translation gives the most common meaning of the word, not just the first dictionary sense.
- Verbs are translated as infinitives, and the aspect (perfective or imperfective) matches the English meaning.
- Adjectives are given in masculine singular.
- Example sentences read as natural, living Ukrainian, not as a word-for-word translation from English.
- No Russianisms or calques from Russian.
